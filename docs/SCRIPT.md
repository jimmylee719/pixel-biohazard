## 2. 無線電：柯瓦奇的主線通話

第一次用無線電：

- KOVAC: "Shen. I left Greycrow on the last flight out, two weeks ago - day three. I'm the one who named this thing. Find the director's records upstairs. I need to know what he did after I left."
  柯瓦奇：「沈若寧。我兩個星期前搭最後一班飛機離開灰鴉島——事件第三天。這東西的名字是我取的。去樓上找站長的紀錄。我要知道我走了之後他做了什麼。」


完成主線「find_shelter」之後：

- KOVAC: "The rules, once. They are blind. They hear a dropped spoon from two rooms away. And they hate the cold - it puts them back to sleep."
  柯瓦奇：「規則我只說一次。它們看不見。隔兩個房間掉一根湯匙它們都聽得到。還有，它們怕冷——冷會讓它們睡回去。」
- KOVAC: "I called it Frostchime. Forty thousand years in the permafrost, and we drilled straight into its bed."
  柯瓦奇：「我叫它霜鳴菌。它在凍土裡睡了四萬年，而我們一鑽就鑽進它的床。」

完成主線「director_records」之後：

- KOVAC: "The Ark. So that's who paid for the drill. Halvorsen told us it was a polar foundation."
  柯瓦奇：「方舟。原來鑽井的錢是他們出的。哈爾沃森跟我們說是一個極地基金會。」
- KOVAC: "He wasn't a monster, Shen. He thought he could keep them asleep and sort it out in the spring. That's worse, in a way."

  船長日誌，北極星號：「困在冰裡第 41 天。方舟的醫生說『病人』不能移動。乘客一律關在客艙。午夜時，劇院裡的歌聲又開始了。」
- NOTE ON THE PIANO: "They held a concert for the sick ones. The sick ones sang back. Nobody clapped."
  鋼琴上的紙條：「他們替生病的人辦了一場音樂會。生病的人唱回來了。沒有人鼓掌。」

### MV Boreal Star - Cargo Hold／北極星號——貨艙

- ARK MANIFEST, BOREAL STAR: "Cargo: 212 passengers (sedated), 38 crew, 6 crates of core ice. Destination: none. The ship IS the destination. Keep it cold. Keep them listening."
  方舟貨物清單，北極星號：「貨物：乘客 212 名（已鎮靜）、船員 38 名、核心冰 6 箱。目的地：無。這艘船本身就是目的地。保持低溫。讓他們一直聽。」

## 6. 天線塔的訊息與回覆

- GREYCROW UPLINK  -  CARRIER LOCKED
  灰鴉上行站　－　載波鎖定
- The tower battery is almost gone. One transmission, then nothing.
  天線塔的電池快沒電了。只能發一次，之後就什麼都沒有。
- Whatever you send decides who comes to this island - and who leaves it.
  你發出的內容，將決定誰會來到這座島——以及誰能離開。
- EVACUATE - send aircraft, get the survivors off the island
  撤離——派飛機來，把倖存者帶離這座島

  [發送] 灰鴉呼叫任何電台。北緯站有倖存者。請求立即撤離。

  [接收] 救援中心：收到，灰鴉。撤離班機已排定，視天候而定。七十二小時。撐住。
- QUARANTINE - seal Greycrow, nobody leaves, nothing lands
  隔離——封鎖灰鴉島，沒有人離開，什麼都不准降落

  [發送] 灰鴉呼叫任何電台。生物汙染。隔離這座島。不要降落。任何人都不准離開。

  [接收] 救援中心：……灰鴉，請再說一次？收到隔離要求。會往上呈報。保持無線電靜默。

  摧毀——研究站必須燒掉，連同裡面的一切
- [OUT] GREYCROW TO ANY STATION. THE STATION IS LOST. DESTROY IT. DO NOT SEND PEOPLE.
  [發送] 灰鴉呼叫任何電台。研究站已經失守。摧毀它。不要派人來。
- [IN] RESCUE CENTRE: Greycrow, we can't act on that. Who is transmitting? Identify yourself.
  [接收] 救援中心：灰鴉，我們無法照辦。發訊的是誰？表明你的身分。

  [接收 126.05] 原地等待。不要再發訊。我們會去接你。

  那不是救援中心。那是 1962 年那捲帶子裡的聲音。研究站底下，鑽探井在嗡嗡作響。

## 7. 結局


### ENDING: QUARANTINE／結局：隔離

- The lift groans up through the dark. By the time you reach the hangar, the sky over Greycrow is full of engines.
  升降機在黑暗中嘎吱上升。等你走到機庫，灰鴉島的天空已經滿是引擎聲。
- Nobody lands. Nobody leaves. Three days later the station burns from the air - exactly as you asked.
  沒有人降落，也沒有人離開。三天後，研究站在空襲中燃燒——正如你所要求的。
- On the fourth day a helicopter with no markings sets down on the south shore. The pilot says your name before you do.
  第四天，一架沒有任何標誌的直升機降落在南岸。你還沒開口，駕駛就先叫出了你的名字。
- You never learn who sent it. Some nights, between stations, your radio plays music from a city that doesn't exist yet.
  你始終不知道是誰派它來的。有些夜裡，收音機在兩個電台之間，會播出一座還不存在的城市的音樂。
- 2099. The city at the end of the century sings with one voice - a little quieter than it should. It is still waiting for you.
  2099 年。世紀末的那座城市用同一個聲音歌唱——比它該有的安靜一點。它還在等你。

### ENDING: EVACUATION／結局：撤離

- The rescue flight comes on the third morning. Blankets, hot tea, a medic who won't stop talking.
  第三天早上，救援班機來了。毯子、熱茶、一個講個不停的醫護兵。
- They take the survivors, the logs, the sealed sample cases. Nobody opens the sample cases.
  他們帶走了倖存者、紀錄，還有密封的樣本箱。沒有人打開那些樣本箱。
- Somewhere over the sea, behind the cargo net, something begins to click.
  在海面上空的某處，貨網後面，有什麼東西開始喀喀作響。
- 2099. The city sings with one voice, and every child learns the name of the courier who brought the song home.
  2099 年。那座城市用同一個聲音歌唱，每個孩子都學過那個把歌帶回家的運送員的名字。

### ENDING: THE LAST LIGHT／結局：最後的光

- You know before the lift stops: there is nobody coming that you could let near this island.
  升降機還沒停下，你就知道了：不管誰要來，都不能讓他們靠近這座島。
- You open every valve on the fuel line and walk back down into the station.
  你打開燃料管線上的每一個閥門，轉身走回研究站。
- From the sea, the rescue crew will report a single bright light on Greycrow. Then, on every frequency, silence.
  海上的救援人員會回報：灰鴉島上有一道很亮的光。然後，每個頻率都安靜了。
- 2099. Nobody builds the city. On 126.05 there is nothing but snow - and once a year, very faintly, a reel-to-reel running out.
  2099 年。沒有人建起那座城市。126.05 上只剩下雪花雜訊——還有每年一次，很微弱地，一捲盤式錄音帶轉到盡頭的聲音。

（已感染時「最後的光」的第一句）

- You know before the lift stops: the cold in your blood isn't the weather anymore.
  升降機還沒停下，你就知道了：血裡的寒意，已經不是天氣造成的。

## 8. 圖鑑


**Watcher／值班者**

- Station staff, still in their coveralls. Blind - the frost took the eyes first - but they hear everything, and they still walk the rounds they walked alive.
  還穿著工作服的站內人員。眼睛最先被霜吃掉，所以看不見；但什麼都聽得到，而且還在走生前巡的那條路線。

**Sleeper／凍眠體**

- A host gone dormant. It lies among the real dead and looks exactly like them - except for the faint glint of crystal, now and then.
  進入休眠的宿主。躺在真正的屍體之間，看起來一模一樣——只有偶爾閃一下的結晶微光不同。

**Chimer／鳴骨者**

- The bone has grown into a resonator. It clicks and listens to the echo, like a bat. Light means nothing to it.
  骨頭長成了共鳴器。它發出喀喀聲、聽回音，像蝙蝠一樣。光對它沒有意義。

**Crawler／爬行者**

- Bent so far forward it walks on its hands. It lives in the ducts and under the floors, and it feels footsteps through the ground.
  身體往前彎到用手走路。住在管道裡、地板下，靠地面傳來的震動感覺腳步。

**Husk／膨脹者**

- The colony has filled this one up like a balloon. Slow, enormous, and one grab carries a frightening dose of spores.
  菌落把它撐得像氣球一樣。又慢又巨大，被抓一下就會吸進可怕份量的孢子。

**Chorister／詠者**

- The crystals ring its throat like a collar. When it sees you, it sings - and everything nearby comes to listen.
  結晶像項圈一樣圍著它的喉嚨。看到你時它會開始唱——附近的東西全都會過來聽。

**Runner／狂奔者**

- Infected only days ago. The frost hasn't slowed it yet - it still runs like the athlete it was, and it hears your heartbeat in the corridor.
  幾天前才被感染。寒冷還沒讓它慢下來——它跑起來還像生前那個運動員，而且聽得到你在走廊上的心跳。

**Spitter／噴孢者**

- Its throat has swollen into a spore sac. It hangs back and spits clots of colony at anything that moves.
  喉嚨腫成了一個孢子囊。它躲在後面，對任何會動的東西吐出一團團菌落。

**Rimebrute／冰甲巨人**

- The colony mineralised this one's bones into a breastplate of ice. Bullets skip off the front. The back is still flesh.
  菌落把它的骨頭礦化成一整片冰的胸甲。子彈打在正面會彈開。背後還是肉。

**Hound／獵犬**

- One of the station's sled dogs. Fast, and its nose still works. It hunts in the open snow.
  研究站的雪橇犬之一。很快，鼻子也還靈。在開闊的雪地裡獵食。

**Tundra Mother／苔原之母**

- A polar bear the colony has made its home. The hangar was hers long before you arrived.
  被菌落當成家的北極熊。在你來之前，機庫就是它的地盤。

**The Deep Well／深井**

- What was waiting at the bottom of the drill shaft. Forty thousand years asleep. Awake now.
  在鑽探井底等著的東西。睡了四萬年。現在醒了。

**Operative／武裝人員**

- Gatekeeper troops, landed to contain the island - and everyone on it. Armed, trained, and they carry radios.
  守門派的部隊，登島來封鎖這座島——連同島上的每一個人。有武器、受過訓練，而且帶著無線電。

**The Warden／典獄長**

- Gate Two's commander. A hunter before the Accord found him. He holds the Boreal Star's sun deck from three perches and never wastes a round - unless you give him a reason.
  守門二號的指揮官。加入協定之前是個獵人。他從三個制高點守著北極星號的頂層甲板，從不浪費一發子彈——除非你給他理由。

**Survival knife／求生刀**

- Your own. Quiet, always there. From behind, one cut is enough.
  你自己的刀。安靜、永遠在身上。從背後一刀就夠了。

**Pistol／手槍**

- Station security sidearm. Eight rounds.
  研究站保全的配槍。8 發。

**Shotgun／散彈槍**

- Pump action. Two shells. Ends arguments at close range.
  泵動式。2 發。近距離一槍解決。

**Hunting rifle／獵槍**

- Scoped, bolt-action. The loudest thing on the island.
  栓動、附瞄準鏡。全島最大聲的東西。

**Nail gun／釘槍**

- Pneumatic, almost silent. Weak, but nothing comes running.
  氣動式，幾乎無聲。威力弱，但不會引來任何東西。

**Flare gun／信號槍**

- Sets the landing spot burning and lights the dark.
  讓落點燃燒，照亮黑暗。

**Fire axe／消防斧**

- Heavy, red, reliable. Carried in place of the knife.
  沉、紅、可靠。拿它就不拿刀。

**Crowbar／鐵撬**

- A metre of hooked steel from the fuel depot. Long reach; puts most things on the floor.
  燃料庫的鐵撬，一公尺長。打得遠，大多數東西一下就倒。

**Revolver／左輪手槍**

- A .357 six-shot from an officer's desk at Gate One. One round stops almost anything.
  守門一號軍官抽屜裡的 .357 六發左輪。一發幾乎什麼都停得下來。

**Submachine gun／衝鋒槍**

- Compact 9mm with a folding stock, Accord issue. Shares ammunition with the pistol.
  協定部隊配發的 9mm 短槍，折疊槍托。和手槍共用子彈。

**Crossbow／十字弓**

- A hunter's crossbow. No report at all, and it hits like a rifle at close range.
  獵人的十字弓。完全沒有槍聲，近距離的威力像步槍。

**Tranquilliser pistol／麻醉槍**

- From the station's kennel. One dart and a grown man sleeps for most of a minute.
  來自研究站的狗舍。一發，一個大男人就會睡上將近一分鐘。

**Tuned radio／調頻收音機**

- A pocket receiver someone soldered onto 126.05 MHz. It plays the song the colony has been singing under the ice since before there were people.
  有人把一台口袋收音機焊死在 126.05 MHz。它放的是菌落在冰層下唱的那首歌——從世上還沒有人類的時候就開始唱了。

**LANTERN carbine／燈籠站卡賓槍**

- Sealed in the recording room since 1962. Factory suppressor, rifle rounds. Somebody expected to need it quietly.
  1962 年起就封在錄音室裡。原廠消音器、步槍彈。當年有人預料到會需要安靜地用它。

**Harpoon gun／捕鯨魚叉槍**

- A whaler's shoulder gun from the old harbour. It throws a bolt hard enough to pin a bear to the ice.
  舊港口捕鯨人的肩射槍。射出去的箭足以把一頭熊釘在冰上。
